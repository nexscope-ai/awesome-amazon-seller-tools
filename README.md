## 🤖 AI Agents & Skills

**The next wave of Amazon seller tools.** AI agents automate research, analysis, and operations — no manual clicking through dashboards. Check each skill’s installation instructions for supported clients such as OpenClaw, Claude Code, Cursor, Windsurf, or Codex.

- [Nexscope Amazon Skills](https://github.com/nexscope-ai/Amazon-Skills) — Reusable skills for Amazon product, keyword, competitor, and market research. Follow the repository’s setup instructions; data access and usage depend on the selected workflow.
  ```bash
  npx skills add nexscope-ai/Amazon-Skills -g
  ```
- [Nexscope](https://www.nexscope.ai/?co-from=github) — Ecommerce marketplace intelligence, online image and video generators, and developer APIs for sellers and ecommerce teams.
  [Explore MCP tools →](https://www.nexscope.ai/mcp?co-from=github&utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=github_mcp_launch&utm_content=awesome-amazon-seller-tools_readme_mcp) · [View API docs →](https://www.nexscope.ai/api-docs?co-from=github&utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=github_mcp_launch&utm_content=awesome-amazon-seller-tools_readme_api)
- [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) and [Claude](https://claude.ai/) for Amazon — General-purpose AI for listing copy, market research, customer response templates, and data analysis.
- [Perplexity for Market Research](https://perplexity.ai/) — AI search engine useful for competitive analysis and trend research.

## All-in-One Platforms

Full-suite tools covering multiple functions — product research, keyword tracking, PPC, listing optimization, and more.

- [Helium 10](https://www.helium10.com/) — Seller toolkit including Cerebro (reverse ASIN), Xray (Chrome extension for product data), AI-powered listing builder, and advertising tools.
- [Jungle Scout](https://www.junglescout.com/) — Offers Catalyst for sellers and Cobalt for brands and enterprise teams. Includes Opportunity Finder, Keyword Scout, Review Automation, Supplier Database, and AI listing builder.
- [Viral Launch](https://viral-launch.com/) — Product discovery, keyword research, market intelligence, and listing optimization. Check current availability and plans with the provider.
- [AMZScout](https://amzscout.net/) — Product research, keyword tracker, and Chrome extension for wholesale, arbitrage, and dropshipping analysis.
- [Perpetua](https://perpetua.io/) — Growth optimization platform combining PPC automation and analytics. Formerly Sellics (merged 2022). Supports Amazon, Walmart, Instacart.
- [SellerApp](https://www.sellerapp.com/) — Product research, keyword tools, PPC analyzer, listing quality checker, and ads automation.
- [ZonGuru](https://www.zonguru.com/) — Helix listing analysis and optimization for supported Amazon marketplaces.
- [DataDive](https://datadive.tools/) — AI-powered all-in-one suite with product research, keyword analysis, and market tracking.

## Product Research

Find profitable products and validate ideas before you invest.

- [Jungle Scout Opportunity Finder](https://www.junglescout.com/features/opportunity-finder/) — Discover high-demand, low-competition niches by filtering categories.
- [Helium 10 Black Box](https://www.helium10.com/tools/product-research/black-box/) — Amazon product database research. Filter by revenue, review count, rating, price, and more.
- [Keepa](https://keepa.com/) — Amazon price tracking and product research. Historical price charts, sales rank tracking, deal alerts. Essential for understanding product history.
- [CamelCamelCamel](https://camelcamelcamel.com/) — Free Amazon price tracker and price drop alert service.
- [Seller Assistant](https://www.sellerassistant.app/) — Shows product data (ROI, profit, restrictions) directly on Amazon pages. Great for wholesale and arbitrage.
- [SmartScout](https://www.smartscout.com/) — Map the entire Amazon marketplace by brand and subcategory. Unique brand-level intelligence.
- [Turbo Piranha](https://www.turbopiranha.com/) — Bulk product search using UPC/ISBN/EAN/ASIN lists. Built for wholesale and arbitrage models.

## Keyword Research

Find the keywords that drive sales on Amazon.

- [Helium 10 Cerebro](https://www.helium10.com/tools/keyword-research/cerebro/) — Reverse ASIN keyword lookup. See exactly which keywords competitors rank for.
- [Helium 10 Magnet](https://www.helium10.com/tools/keyword-research/magnet/) — Forward keyword research with search volume, competition score, and related keywords.
- [Jungle Scout Keyword Scout](https://www.junglescout.com/features/keyword-scout/) — Keyword research with historical search volume trends and PPC bid data.
- [Keywords.am](https://keywords.am/) — Amazon keyword intent and listing optimization tools.
- [MerchantWords](https://www.merchantwords.com/) — Keyword database across global Amazon marketplaces with search volume data.
- [Keyword Tool for Amazon](https://keywordtool.io/amazon) — Generate keyword ideas from Amazon autocomplete.

## Listing Optimization

Write better titles, bullets, descriptions, and A+ content.

- [Jungle Scout AI Listing Builder](#all-in-one-platforms) — AI generates optimized listings from your keywords.
- [SellerApp Listing Quality](#all-in-one-platforms) — Score your listing and get specific improvement recommendations.

## PPC & Advertising

Manage and optimize Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, and DSP campaigns.

- [Teikametrics](https://www.teikametrics.com/) — AI-powered PPC optimization using predictive bidding. Supports Amazon + Walmart.
- [Pacvue](https://www.pacvue.com/) — Enterprise advertising platform for Amazon, Walmart, and Instacart. AI-powered bid optimization.
- [Perpetua](#all-in-one-platforms) — Automated advertising optimization with goal-based campaigns.

- [AiHello](https://www.aihello.com/) — PPC automation with AI autopilot bidding.
- [Advigator](https://www.advigator.com/) — Simple, automated Amazon PPC management.
- [SellerMate.ai](https://www.sellermate.ai/) — AI-powered PPC automation and business reports.
- [BidX](https://www.bidx.io/) — Amazon advertising optimization platform with automated bidding.

Get more reviews, manage feedback, and monitor customer sentiment.

## Pricing & Repricing

Dynamic pricing to stay competitive and win the Buy Box.

- [RepricerExpress](https://www.repricerexpress.com/) — AI-powered repricing. Adjusts prices in real-time based on competition, Buy Box status, and custom rules.
- [BQool](https://www.bqool.com/) — Scheduled repricing with Buy Box competition strategies.
- [Informed.co](https://www.informed.co/) — Machine-learning repricer for Amazon, eBay, Walmart, and more.
- [Aura](https://goaura.com/) — AI repricing focused on Buy Box winning strategies.

## Inventory Management

Forecast demand, manage stock levels, and avoid stockouts.

- [SoStocked](https://www.sostocked.com/) — Amazon inventory management and demand forecasting by Carbon6. Customizable forecasting models.
- [RestockPro](https://www.ecomengine.com/restockpro) — FBA inventory management with restock suggestions and shipment planning.
- [Sellerboard](https://sellerboard.com/) — Profit analytics with built-in inventory management and restock alerts.
- [InventoryLab](https://www.threecolts.com/seller-365/inventorylab) — All-in-one listing, accounting, and inventory management. Popular with FBA sellers.

Understand your true profit after all Amazon fees, PPC costs, and COGS.

- [Sellerboard](#inventory-management) — Real-time profit dashboard with fee breakdown, PPC cost tracking, and trend analysis.
- [HelloProfit](https://helloprofit.com/) — Multi-marketplace profit dashboard with customizable views.

## Reimbursements & Refunds

Recover money Amazon owes you from lost/damaged inventory, overcharged fees, etc.

- [GETIDA](https://getida.com/) — FBA reimbursement auditing. Files claims on your behalf. Pay only on successful recovery.
- [Threecolts Margin Pro](https://www.threecolts.com/margin-pro) — Auditing and recovery for marketplace reimbursements and other margin losses; the former Refund Sniper domain redirects here.
- [Seller Investigators](https://sellerinvestigators.com/) — FBA audit and reimbursement recovery service.

Professional product images and A+ content design.

- [Canva](https://www.canva.com/) — Design tool for infographics, A+ content, and storefront images.

## Sourcing & Suppliers

Find manufacturers and manage your supply chain.

- [Alibaba](https://www.alibaba.com/) — B2B marketplace for finding manufacturers and suppliers.
- [Jungle Scout Supplier Database](https://www.junglescout.com/features/supplier-database/) — Search global supplier records, see which factories supply top Amazon brands.
- [ImportYeti](https://www.importyeti.com/) — Free tool to find suppliers of any company using public US customs data.
- [1688.com](https://www.1688.com/) — Alibaba's domestic (China) marketplace. Primarily serves the domestic Chinese market.

## Market Intelligence & Monitoring

Track market trends, competitor moves, and category shifts.

- [Keepa](#product-research) — Historical price and BSR tracking. The gold standard for product history data.
- [Nexscope Monitor](https://github.com/hhw19970223/nexscope-monitor) — Open-source global e-commerce intelligence dashboard. Real-time market data visualization.
- [SentryKit](https://sentrykit.com/) — Product monitoring and alerts for Amazon sellers. Track listing changes, hijackers, and Buy Box status.
- [Flapen](https://flapen.com/) — AI-driven Amazon brand management and product research services.
- [DataHawk](#analytics--profit-tracking) — Market intelligence with competitive tracking and trend analysis.
